What does parallel mean inside one QPU?

Imagine one instruction that adds one to each of sixteen numbers. The grid shows the programmer’s sixteen lanes. A lane is one position in that group, with its own input and result. How many physical arithmetic lanes would you need if you could reuse them?

One 64-bit QPU instruction stored as two 32-bit words · v3d_lists.S, line 66

.word 0xffffcd28, 0xe00208e7    // ldi r3, packed fragment color

The two hexadecimal words in Gabix encode one load-immediate instruction. Its destination is r3. The first word supplies the literal value. The second encodes the operation and its destination. Broadcom guide · p. 26
