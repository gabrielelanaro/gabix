Who runs a shader, and who sets up its inputs?

Suppose the driver changes just the color returned by a small GPU program. Predict whether the triangle’s edge moves. To decide, follow the hardware that prepares work for that program.

The same QPUs can also run vertex programs earlier in a typical draw. Our driver supplies prepared vertices in Broadcom’s NV mode. Gabix supplies screen coordinates directly, so V3D can set up this triangle without running a vertex shader first. Broadcom guide · p. 63
