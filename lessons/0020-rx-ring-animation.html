A few things to try. Hit Burst of 4 without polling in between: the supply drops to 4 posted. Hit it again: supply hits 0, the next frame gets dropped, the red banner appears. Hit Kernel polls a few times to drain the backlog. Then turn on Auto-play and let the system find its steady state.

The descriptor cells never move — only their colors change. The buffer memory they point at is the same 2048 bytes forever. What flows is state: posted → filled → reading → posted, around and around. The avail and used rings are how the kernel and device tell each other whose turn it is to look at each buffer.
