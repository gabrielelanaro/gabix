The fragment shader in Gabix

Track the packed foreground from the input bytes to the tile-color destination in the last few QPU instructions.

Patch one instruction

A nine-byte draw supplies four background bytes and four foreground bytes after the operation byte. With a five-byte draw, the ARM takes the default gold. It patches the low 32-bit word of the load-immediate instruction.

Follow r3

Each QPU instruction occupies two 32-bit words here. The patched immediate is loaded into r3. The shader waits for the scoreboard, writes r3 to tile color, and releases the scoreboard in the final delay slot.

Check against the board
