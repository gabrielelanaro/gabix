Read the vertex data and the independent check on the Mac. These are the two ends of the same draw.

Decode the top corner

The assembler stores two 16-bit coordinates. Dividing by 16 gives (32, 8). The following words supply depth, reciprocal W and three varying values. Each vertex occupies 24 bytes.

Predict the second pixel

The host computes the center comparison using integers. On row 32 the right side of the inequality is 49. For column 19 the left side is 50. For column 20 it is 46. Read the next line to locate those results in the file.

Check against the board

In the boot 32 gold frame, columns 19 and 20 on row 32 contain 96 42 19 ff and 28 cd ff ff. Move to row 33 and both contain gold. Frames tested
