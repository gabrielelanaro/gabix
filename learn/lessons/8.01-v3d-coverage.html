Why does one pixel turn gold?

The Pi has cleared a 64 × 64 image to blue. Give it three corners and gold. On row 32, which of two neighbors changes when the triangle is drawn?

The driver supplies screen coordinates for the top corner (32, 8), lower left (8, 56), and lower right (56, 56). Screen y increases downward. The left edge therefore moves half a pixel to the left for every row you move down. Three vertex records

What the hardware calculates

V3D’s rasterizer takes a triangle and computes coverage at sample positions. Coverage lets the GPU apply the fragment color only where the triangle overlaps those samples. The three vertices in this draw are already in screen space. For this single-sample target, the Mac checks coverage at each pixel center. Screen-space draw · Pixel-center test

Read the top vertex

The first four bytes are 00 02 80 00. Split them into two little-endian halfwords. They encode 512 and 128. Four fractional bits put the corner at (32, 8).
